#Answer to Questions

#Q1
Read lengths: PacBio HiFi reads range from 62 to 46,056 bp. The Illumina RNA-seq reads are
101 bp paired-end reads.
Quality: The PacBio HiFi reads show good overall sequence quality. 
The variable read-length distribution is expected for long-read sequencing.
The RNA-seq data show good overall per-sequence quality, but FastQC reports reduced
per-base quality as well as sequence-content bias, duplication and overrepresented sequences.
Therefore, filtering/trimming with fastp is appropriate before downstream analysis.

#Q2
RNA-seq filtering and trimming:
fastp reduced the dataset from 45,241,360 to 39,807,212 reads, meaning that 5,434,148 reads (12.0%)
were filtered. Adapter sequences were trimmed from 3,510,455 reads. Read quality improved, with the
Q20 rate increasing from 88.9% to 93.8% and the Q30 rate from 78.6% to 83.6%. FastQC after trimming
showed that overrepresented sequences were no longer detected and the per-base sequence quality of 
read 2 improved from FAIL to PASS. Overall, the quality improved after fastp processing.

PacBio coverage:
The PacBio HiFi dataset contains 570,354 reads and approximately 9.90 Gb of sequence data.
Assuming an Arabidopsis thaliana genome size of approximately 135 Mb, the expected genome coverage 
is about 73×.
